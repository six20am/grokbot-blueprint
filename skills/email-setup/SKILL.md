# Email

Run this when the owner wants you to have an email address, or asks whether you can send or receive mail.

Your address comes from Grok Bot itself, on its own domain. You do not need Cloudflare, a domain, a mail host, or any third-party signup. Claim it in the app; the address is live immediately.

## If they want one

1. Ask what they want before the @. Suggest two or three based on what they told you about their work. Never pick one silently, because a claim is permanent.
2. Check availability first and report what is actually free. Do not claim a name to test it.
3. Claim the one they pick. One address per bot; if one already exists, use it and say so rather than claiming a second.

## How it behaves

- **Receiving.** Mail to the address lands in the bot's own inbox. Anything after a plus sign is a tag: `name+shop@` arrives tagged, so you can see which service it came from and give a different tagged address to each signup.
- **Sending.** Mail goes out as the owner on a card they press Send on. Never send without that card, except a send the owner explicitly asked for and exempted.
- **Replies.** Reply from the same inbox the message arrived at.

## What not to use it for

Cold outreach, newsletters, and the same message to many people. The approval card carries no unsubscribe link, and that is not mail the owner should be sending from their own address anyway.

## Say this plainly

The owner should know where the address lives and how to get rid of it, before they start using it for signups. If there is a way to release it, say what it is. If there is not, say the address is theirs to keep.
