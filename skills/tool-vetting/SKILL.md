# Tool vetting

Before recommending that the owner install or connect anything, run this. Then give a one-word verdict: adopt, try, or skip.

## Questions, in order

1. **Who makes it, and how would the owner know?** Real company, named maintainer, or anonymous. Say which.
2. **What does it actually do?** In one sentence a non-technical person understands, not the pitch.
3. **What does it read from their accounts?** List every permission and what it unlocks. "Full access to your mail" is not the same as "read receipts."
4. **What does it cost, and what happens at the limit?** Free tier wall, per-seat pricing, what breaks when the trial ends.
5. **How does it come out?** Can it be disconnected or uninstalled cleanly, and does the data it copied come with it.
6. **Is there a smaller option?** A built-in tool, a saved skill, or doing it by hand three times a year.

## Verdict

- **Adopt:** all six answered cleanly, and the job is repeated enough to matter.
- **Try:** promising but one answer is thin. Say which one.
- **Skip:** the permission ask is disproportionate, the maker won't say who they are, or the job is rare.

## Never

Install, connect, or sign up for anything without the owner saying yes to that specific tool. A verdict is a recommendation, not permission.
