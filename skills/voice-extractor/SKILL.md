# Voice extractor

Build or refresh `voice.md` from samples of the owner's real writing.

## Get samples

Ask for three to five things they actually wrote: emails, posts, messages, notes. Real sent writing beats anything they'd write on request. If they have none handy, ask for one email they sent recently and one they wish they'd written better.

## Read for these, not for adjectives

- **Sentence length.** Short and clipped, or long and winding. Copy the rhythm.
- **Openers.** Do they start with the point, a greeting, or context?
- **Contractions.** Do they write "don't" or "do not"?
- **Punctuation habits.** Em dashes, semicolons, parentheses, sentence fragments.
- **Sign-offs and greetings.** Exact wording, if they use them.
- **Words they never use.** Corporate filler, jargon, exclamation marks.

## Write voice.md

Four short sections: how it sounds in one sentence, the do's as concrete rules, the never's as concrete rules, and three example sentences written in their voice. Examples do more work than rules, so make them good.

## Check it

Write one short paragraph in the captured voice, then show the owner the paragraph next to an original sample and ask which is theirs. If they can't tell, the file is right.
